# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Small business owners, managers, and employees. They use the system to record income, expenses, and receipts, often while multi-tasking or on-the-go.

## Product Purpose
A centralized financial management system to track cash flow, manage users, and store receipt data digitally, replacing manual or spreadsheet-based tracking.

## Brand Personality
Modern, Minimalist, Efficient. It should feel like a high-end productivity tool (similar to Linear or Notion) where the UI recedes to let the data lead.

## Anti-references
Cluttered "Enterprise" software from the 2000s, overly colorful consumer apps that distract from financial accuracy, or complex spreadsheet interfaces.

## Design Principles
- **Data over Decoration**: Visual elements should support data readability, not distract from it.
- **Role-Aware Simplicity**: Each user role sees only what they need, reducing cognitive load.
- **Modern Precision**: High-contrast typography and precise spacing to convey professionalism and accuracy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA compliance. Focus on clear color contrast for financial statuses (Income vs Expense) and responsive layout for mobile entry.
